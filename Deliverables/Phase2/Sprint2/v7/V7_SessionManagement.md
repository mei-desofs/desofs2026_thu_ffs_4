## Implementation Notes

- Session constants are centralized in `CantinasApp/Backend/src/Config/session.ts`.
- Session persistence is handled by `CantinasApp/Backend/src/Model/UserSession.ts`.
- Session verification and revocation are handled by `CantinasApp/Backend/src/Service/SessionService.ts`.
- Authentication now validates the token and then verifies the backend session record in `CantinasApp/Backend/src/middlewares/authMiddleware.ts`.
- Login, logout, password-change, password-reset, quarantine, and admin session termination flows are exposed through `CantinasApp/Backend/src/Controller/UserController.ts` and `CantinasApp/Backend/src/Routes/UserRoutes.ts`.
